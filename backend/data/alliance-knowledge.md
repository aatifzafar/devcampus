## Marks and assessment
Each theory subject is marked out of 100. Continuous internal evaluation (CIE) is 50 marks: mid-term test 1 (15), mid-term test 2 (15), assignments (10) and quizzes (10). The semester-end examination (SEE) is 50 marks. To pass, a student needs at least 40% in the SEE and 40% overall. Lab subjects use weekly records, a viva, a lab assessment and an end-semester practical.

## Grades and CGPA
Grades are awarded on a 10-point scale: O (outstanding), A+, A, B+, B, C and F. SGPA is the credit-weighted average for one semester; CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all completed semesters. Results are published on the platform under My progress.

## Tests on the platform
Quizzes and some mid-terms are taken online under Tests. Proctored tests need a laptop with a working camera; a short camera and connection check runs before the test starts. Answers save automatically. Leaving the test tab is noted for faculty review but does not end the test. If the connection drops, reopen the test from Tests → Live now to continue.

## Assignments
Assignments are submitted under My subjects → Assignments before the deadline. Work can be resubmitted until the deadline; the latest file counts. Late submissions are accepted but marked late, and the faculty decides any penalty.

## Re-evaluation
After results are published, a student may apply for re-evaluation of the end-semester answer script through the examination cell within the window announced with the results.

## Library
The central library is open to all students with their university ID card. Books can be borrowed for 14 days and renewed once if no one else has reserved them. E-resources such as journal databases are available on campus and through the library portal.

## Mentoring
Every student has a faculty mentor, shown in Settings. Mentors meet their mentees at least twice a semester and can be contacted for academic or personal concerns.

## Placements and internships
The placement cell announces internship and placement drives on the platform and by university email. Students must register for each drive before its deadline and keep their resume updated. Eligibility criteria (CGPA, backlogs) are set per company and listed with each drive.

## Clubs and events
Students can join technical and cultural clubs at the start of each semester. Hackathons, workshops and fests are announced on the platform under What's new and by email.

## Help and contacts
For platform problems, use Help → Give feedback → Report a problem. For examination matters, contact the examination cell. For fee matters, contact the accounts office.
